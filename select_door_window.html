About Select Door & Window

A family-owned, licensed contractor serving Escondido and North County San Diego since 2008.

Our Story

Built on One Simple Promise

Select Door and Window was founded on a straightforward idea: if we sell it, we install it, and we service it. No middlemen, no subcontractors, no finger-pointing if something isn't right.

Since 2008, we've been serving homeowners and businesses across Escondido and North County San Diego with that same commitment. From a single entry door replacement to a full window package, our team handles every step in-house.

We're a certified Andersen dealer and carry products from Milgard, La Cantina, Marvin, EL&EL, and TM Cobb — so whether you're looking for a classic wood door, an energy-efficient window, or a dramatic folding wall system, we have the right product and the expertise to install it correctly.

Built on Reputation

Select Door & Window is a family-run company, founded in Escondido in 2008. We started with a simple belief: that homeowners deserve a contractor who treats their project like it's their own home being worked on.

Over 15 years later, most of our new business still comes from referrals. That's not an accident — it's the result of showing up on time, doing the work right, and standing behind it after the job is done.

When you call us, you're talking to the people who will actually be on your project. Not a call center, not a franchise — just us.
